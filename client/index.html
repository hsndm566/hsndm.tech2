Organise your Saudi job search with AutoApply SA

AutoApply SA helps job seekers across Saudi Arabia organise their search, review CV skills, choose target roles and cities, and keep opportunities and next actions in one place. The service is available in Arabic and English.

How the job-search workspace helps

Start by reviewing experience from your CV, then set the kinds of roles and locations you want to explore. Save relevant opportunities, record applications you submit yourself, and return to a clear list of next steps.

What is available today

CV review and keyword suggestions help you understand your experience. The workspace supports preference setting and manual opportunity tracking. Automated employer submission is not currently enabled, and the public preview does not submit applications for you.

For job seekers and agents

AutoApply SA is designed for candidates seeking work in Saudi Arabia, including new graduates and professionals considering a career move. The public API provides read-only product information, plan details, and service health; it does not expose candidate profiles or application submission.
