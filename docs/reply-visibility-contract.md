# Reply visibility contract

The dashboard may show an outbound application as **email accepted** only after transport evidence is recorded for that candidate's application.

The dashboard may show **employer confirmation/reply** only after an inbound mail provider, webhook, or audited operator action records a candidate-scoped employer confirmation event.

Do not infer replies from outbound delivery. Do not expose another candidate's mailbox or application evidence. Do not claim inbox synchronization until the inbound integration is active and tested.
