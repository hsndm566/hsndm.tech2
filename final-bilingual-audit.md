# Final Bilingual Audit

## Scope

The audit compared the English and Arabic landing pages’ public sections, prices, Saudi location options, contact references, privacy boundary, readiness behavior, language toggle, and legacy geography terms. It also reviewed the live RTL and mobile presentation.

## Confirmed parity

| Area | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Public landing-page sections | Both routes use the same eight anchored sections: product, workflow, upload, campaign example, pricing, reviews, FAQ, and location. |
| Pricing | Both routes publish the same tiers: **99**, **149**, and **249 SAR**. |
| Saudi focus | Both routes use the same Saudi city options and contain no Gulf-wide or other-country legacy positioning. |
| Contact routing | Both routes use the configured `966571448656` WhatsApp endpoint and `hasan@hsndm.tech` contact address. |
| CV privacy boundary | Both routes perform browser-local CV text extraction and communicate that the CV file and text are not sent by the readiness check. |
| Readiness flow | English and Arabic each provide a localized readiness preview, loading state, completion state, and WhatsApp handoff. |

## Fact-validation boundary

The selectable cities **Jeddah, Riyadh, Dammam, Makkah, and Al-Madinah Al-Munawwarah** are valid Saudi cities. Saudipedia identifies Riyadh as the capital of Riyadh Province, Makkah al-Mukarramah and Al-Madinah al-Munawwarah as administrative capitals of their provinces, and Dammam as the capital of the Eastern Province. The Digital Government Authority framework supports mobile-first, beneficiary-centric and bilingual digital experiences. [1] [2]

Commercial terms, target application volumes, payment availability, response timing, the operator’s Jeddah base, supplied reviews, and WhatsApp ownership are **owner-provided service information**. They can be made consistent across languages, but cannot be independently verified from public sources; the audit therefore did not convert them into claims of guaranteed outcomes.

## Result

No confirmed bilingual mismatch, stale non-Saudi geography, inconsistent price, or broken English/Arabic flow was found. No content correction is required. The only release change is the repeatable audit script and this recorded audit evidence.
